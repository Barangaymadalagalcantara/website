Officials with no end year are shown as current on the website. Past terms appear automatically in the Timeline of Past Officials. Photos are shown in a gold picture frame (portrait 4:5, cropped automatically).

These appear in the Emergency Information section and the Emergency Hotlines pop-up on the website. When an agency changes its number, click the pencil to update it. Numbers are tap-to-call on phones.

Fill in the figures for your barangay. Anything left empty is hidden on the website, so nothing looks unfinished. Use aggregate numbers only (counts, never names or personal details of residents). Starter figures come from the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA); replace them with your Registry of Barangay Inhabitants / CBMS data when you have it.

Editors can manage announcements, ordinances & resolutions, and barangay officials. Admins can also handle requests/concerns, and manage this list. The person must first have an account (Supabase → Authentication → Users → Add user).
